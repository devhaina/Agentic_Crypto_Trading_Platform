About these figures

Built from order fill events with average-cost accounting, and marked against the Market Data Service's latest cached price — the same cache the risk gate reads. Realised P&L is net of fees paid in the quote asset; a fee paid in another asset is not converted and is excluded.

Every position here is read against one fixed, caller-supplied trading account id — there is no Identity Service yet, so this page is not scoped to a signed-in user. See docs/architecture/known-limitations.md.
